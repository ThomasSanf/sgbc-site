# Cartridge collection photograph

The “Keep the cartridges” section uses a photograph of SGBC’s own collection:
Pokémon Green, Crystal, Emerald, Sapphire, and Ruby. Each visible shell was
traced at the resolution of the original photograph. The cartridge labels,
contacts, and plastic are photographic pixels.

The portrait HEIC (3024 × 4032 after orientation) was used directly rather than
enlarging the previous web crop. The five cutouts share a mild color correction
and sharpening pass. They overlap where the camera could not see an edge;
no missing artwork was reconstructed. The background is transparent, with
soft shadows between layers and a subtle glow supplied by the page.

The two sRGB WebP exports are `sgbc-cartridge-lineup-960.webp` (960 × 540)
and `sgbc-cartridge-lineup-2160.webp` (2160 × 1215). Only the web copies
are published; neither contains camera or location metadata.
